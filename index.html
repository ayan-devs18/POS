Users and passwords

Owner: full rights, including menu, prices, stock, reports, credit, voids and settings. Cashier: only takes orders and prints bills (and can reprint a past bill). Nothing else.

Sales tax

The card rate applies to debit and credit cards, mobile wallets (JazzCash, Easypaisa) and QR payments. Credit (udhaar) bills use the cash rate. Rates change with each provincial budget, so confirm them with the restaurant's accountant.

Data

All bills, payments, menu and stock are saved in a database on this computer. Download an Excel copy or a backup at the end of every day.
